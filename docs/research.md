## Studio COKA

- **What:** a design-build studio working across architecture, interior design and urban design.
- **Where:** Enugu, Nigeria, working across Africa and internationally.
- **Positioning:** climate-responsive, high-performance buildings that reduce energy demand and improve comfort. Homepage line: "Built for this climate. Designed for people."
- **Services:** design and build; architecture design only; interior design.
- **Process:** seven stages, from briefing to handover.
- **Stated results:** up to 70% less energy demand and up to 90% less cooling; designed for off-grid performance.
- **Website:** built with Next.js.

### TESH Nsukka (Nigeria's first off-grid hospital)

- Fully solar powered, independent of the national grid.
- 400% increase in patient visits; 95% reduction in diesel use.
- About ₦8M saved each year on energy; around 32 tonnes of CO₂ avoided annually.
- A raised central atrium and perimeter openings ventilate the waiting area naturally.

Studio COKA's studio page credits the hospital's design to Crystal before the studio was founded, while the homepage presents it as a case study. The landing page therefore attributes it to Crystal and links to the case study.
